  `NavigationMenuItem.js` and their generated themes are the pinned reference. Base behavior comes from
  `@ui5/webcomponents/dist/Menu.js`, `MenuItem.js`, `ListItemTemplate.js`, `Popup.js` and `Popover.js`.

  The live SideNavigation probe recorded the real associated item click before the canceled parent
  opened and before the canceled child closed the tree. The standalone React callback uses that
  associated-item order. Its destination anchor retains the native href and target default action.
  Evidence `.integration/H10/probe.log` and `.integration/H10/associated.log`.

  list topology, row labels, states, description, text and relationship targets. `Popup.js` sets
  `aria-modal=true` even for the nonmodal desktop popup. `NavigationMenuItem.js/_accInfo` forces
  `role=none` on a focusable LI, which Chromium reports as a listitem and duplicates beneath its
  anchor where present. Petalo owns one menuitem focus target, keeps the native anchor where applicable,
  and represents the tag as a description. The corrected shape preserves the item states and text. This
  allowance is restricted to the three named in-place root cases. The raw AX captures are retained.
