# H5 AnalyticalTable acceptance

H5 adds WCR row and column virtualisation controls `overscanCount` and `overscanCountHorizontal`, plus `nativeScrollbar` on the existing viewport. Header and body scroll together with frozen columns. The `tableRef` retains WCR `scrollTo`, `scrollToItem`, `horizontalScrollTo` and `horizontalScrollToItem`. The performance case steps through 100,000 rows and 50 columns with a bounded mounted range.

Infinite loading uses `infiniteScroll`, `infiniteScrollThreshold`, `onLoadMore` and `onTableScroll`. `totalRowCount` reports the known server total or `-1` when unknown. WCR's `VirtualTableBodyContainer.js` checks the visible-row threshold and also checks the bottom for grouped rows. Petalo uses the measured virtual row range and rearms on the loaded core row count, including appends to an existing collapsed group, and limits requests to one per loaded count.

Responsive columns use `responsiveMinWidth`, `responsivePopIn`, `popinDisplay`, `adjustTableHeightOnPopIn` and the experimental `PopInHeader` content slot. The Custom example renders a custom heading and retains the cell's owned accessible references. The hostile consumer test checks that the cell preserves its role, row and column references, ID and tabindex.

The equivalent of `useF2CellEdit` is `f2CellEdit` with `interactiveElementName`. F2 moves into the editor. F2 and Escape return to the cell. Tab moves through controls in the mounted editors and between a focused header and its body column. The existing resize callback `onColumnResize` and `announceEmptyCells` equivalent remain covered.

## Paired evidence

The seven new WCR fixtures cover the middle of 10,000 rows, frozen horizontal scrolling, three pop-in modes, a focused editor and a loaded table fetching another page. All 63 added visual cells matched in dimensions across nine theme, density and direction combinations. The largest pixel difference was 1.4754%, in High Contrast White compact loading. Both LTR and RTL WCR accessibility trees passed. No stored tree-baseline entry was added.

For the loaded busy case, WCR's `AnalyticalTable/index.js` exposes a focusable indeterminate progress indicator before the grid. H5 keeps its visual overlay and exposes the indicator's localized text in a dedicated busy row with `aria-busy` on the grid. The case-specific comparison validates the exact progress indicator, busy grid and named busy row before comparing all loaded row names, cells, states and relations.

Petalo's fixture axe check passed in four themes and both directions. WCR's `PopIn.js` emits adjacent visible DOM fragments, such as `Alpine TentType:Shelter`. WCR's `useA11y.js` joins its IDREFs with spaces. Axe reports this native-only difference in the five first-column cells of each of the three pop-in fixtures. The test asserts the raw rule, role, markup and exact fixture/row set.

The 100,000 × 50 benchmark completed 32 scroll steps in 549.9 ms in the final main run, with at most 14 rows and 13 columns mounted. A new shadcn app requested `@petalo/analytical-table` and imported its item entry. It passed strict typecheck, production build and headless F2, responsive naming and infinite loading checks.
